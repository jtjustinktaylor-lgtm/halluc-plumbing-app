# 🔧 Halluc Plumbing — Business Manager

A complete business management PWA for plumbing professionals.

**Features:**
- 📊 Dashboard with business overview
- 📅 Job scheduling & calendar
- 💰 Quotes & invoicing
- 👥 Customer management
- 📷 Job photo capture (auto-compressed)
- 🛡️ Warranty tracking
- 🔧 Maintenance plans
- 📧 Email follow-ups
- 📱 Works offline (PWA)
- 🖨️ Print-ready documents

**Live:** [halluc-plumbing-app.vercel.app](https://halluc-plumbing-app.vercel.app)

Built with vanilla JS — no build step, no dependencies.
